# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients, collaborators, design-conscious engineering teams, and hiring managers evaluating Izuchukwu Tony's product engineering work. They need to understand his point of view, technical range, shipped work, and availability quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present Izuchukwu as a software engineer and digital product builder whose technical execution is matched by strong visual judgment. Success means visitors can identify what he does, inspect credible project work, understand his experience, and contact him without friction.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, art-directed. The experience should feel calm but memorable, technical without developer clichés, and expressive without becoming ornamental.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, dashboard-style portfolio grids, glassmorphism, gradient-heavy themes, identical rounded cards, fabricated testimonials or awards, excessive parallax, and animations that interfere with native scrolling.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with evidence: real project imagery and specific outcomes carry more weight than claims.
2. Typography establishes identity: scale, rhythm, and placement should make the name unforgettable.
3. Motion supports reading: reveals and marquees provide continuity without slowing navigation.
4. Keep the path obvious: identity, work, experience, capabilities, then contact.
5. Make mobile intentional: preserve the visual idea while simplifying computation and interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, descriptive image alternatives, touch targets of at least 44px, and a polished reduced-motion path.
